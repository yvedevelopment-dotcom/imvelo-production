About Imvelo Monitoring System

Discover the stories, science, and soul of Eswatini’s indigenous trees.

Overview

Imvelo is a comprehensive, database-driven platform for cataloging and exploring the indigenous trees of Eswatini. It provides users with in-depth information about each tree, including its ecological role, cultural and medicinal significance, conservation status, and connections to heritage sites.

Key Features

  • Tree Listings: Browse and search a rich collection of indigenous trees.
  • Detailed Profiles: View images, benefits, and conservation efforts.
  • Categories: Explore trees by ecological roles or cultural significance.
  • Heritage Sites: Discover sites and their linked tree species.
  • Conservation Status: Stay informed about each species’ protection level.
  • Newsletter: Subscribe to get updates on new data and conservation news.

User Guide

  • Homepage: The landing page features the latest trees; use the search bar to find specific species.
  • Tree Details: Click any tree to view its full profile, images, and benefits.
  • Categories & Heritage: Filter trees by category or explore heritage sites.
  • Conservation: Learn about ongoing efforts and statuses.
  • Subscribe: Enter your email to receive updates.
